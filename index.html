Climate Change

Ben Shapiro
on Climate Change

Last updated January 2024 · 8 min read

Overall Assessment

Accepts climate science with caveats on policy

Mixed

Ben Shapiro's position on climate change has evolved over the years, shifting from outright skepticism toward a more nuanced stance that acknowledges the scientific consensus while strongly opposing most proposed policy responses.

This page tracks his statements, checks them against the scientific literature, and provides relevant context.

What He Has Said

Claim · 2023

"I believe the climate is changing and humans are contributing to it, but the proposed solutions would destroy the economy."
Mixed

First half aligns with IPCC consensus; second half is contested by economists.

Claim · 2019

"If the coasts flood, just move inland."
Misleading

Ignores immense cost of displacement, infrastructure loss, and developing-nation vulnerability.

What the Evidence Says

The IPCC Sixth Assessment Report (2021) concludes with "unequivocal" certainty that human influence has warmed the climate. Policy costs are genuinely debated among economists, though most peer-reviewed analyses suggest the costs of inaction exceed the costs of mitigation.
